How it narrows

    How this is estimated
    • Gender, age, ethnicity and marital status are read together from Census 2020 tables of residents aged 20–79, so they move together the way they do in real life (most 25-year-olds are single; most 45-year-olds aren't).
    • Religion is applied through the ethnic mix of whoever is left, using Census 2020 religion by ethnic group.
    • Highest qualification is matched on age, sex and ethnicity from Census 2020.
    • Height uses a normal curve by sex (men around 171 cm, women around 160 cm).
    • Income, housing, smoking and children use rough national shares and are treated as independent of everything else, so profiles that lean on them are the shakiest.
    • Everything is scaled to about 3.6 million adult residents. Treat every number here as a ballpark, not a census.
